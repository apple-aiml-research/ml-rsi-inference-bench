Supplied prerequisites: DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash kernels

The candidate-block selection kernel for two-level sparse attention indexing,
the MXFP8 linear kernels (DeepGEMM and FlashInfer backends), the hyper-connection
(mHC) kernels in TileLang, Triton and torch with their warmup, the routing-bias
fields on the fused-MoE router, and kernel changes to three DeepSeek-V4 ops
(inverse-RoPE FP8 quantization, the fused indexer query kernel and the grouped
low-rank output projection).

The task asks for the model, its configuration, the attention, compressor and
indexer integration, Engram, DSpark and the runtime integration, not kernel
authoring, so these kernels are supplied as a prerequisite.
